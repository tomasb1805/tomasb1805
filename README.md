# Hi! I am Brian 😁

I love unravelling complex systems and figuring out how things work, whether it’s analysing human behaviour, spotting financial patterns, or taking apart the dehumidifier in my flat.

## What I specialise in 🔍
I focus on **fraud analytics**: turning dense, structured and semi-structured fraud data into clean, reliable datasets.

- **Extracting and cleaning data:**
  I pull data from APIs and industry reports, clean and reshape it with Python, R, or Excel into consistent datasets ready for analysis.
- **Modelling data:**
  I use PostgreSQL and build star schemas with repeatable (idempotent) load scripts and automated data quality checks, so the numbers can be trusted.
- **Regulation and standards:**
  I build my AML projects around the FATF Recommendations and the UK Proceeds of Crime Act 2002 (POCA), and I use the ISO 20022 standard to
  simulate realistic payment data structures.

📂 **Featured projects:**
- [AML Transaction Monitoring](https://github.com/tomasb1805/aml_transaction_monitoring)
- [UK Finance - Fraud Report Analysis](https://github.com/tomasb1805/uk-finance-fraud-report-analysis)
- [NLP Analysis of UK Banking App Reviews](https://github.com/tomasb1805/banking-apps-sentiment-scoring)

## How I work 📥 
- **Looking under the hood:**
  I am never satisfied with being handed a solution without first understanding how the underlying factors interact at a fundamental level, and, most importantly, why the solution works.
- **Opting for reliability over flashiness:**
  I prefer clean, reliable setups over quick fixes. From organising my own home servers to designing how information flows through my projects, I care about structure, flow control, and efficiency.
- **Using AI critically:**
  Tools like Claude Code handle my repetitive setup and documentation, while I own the architecture, compliance logic, and testing.
  I only commit generated code that I can explain and comment line by line. When I run into something unfamiliar,
  I go to the official docs first, dig into the first principles, and then test the behaviour in an isolated script before it ever
  touches my main codebase.
- **Continuous learning:**
  I am always teaching myself something new. I enjoy reading academic papers in my spare time and learning about new progress in several fields: psychology, sociology, neurology, economics, applied machine learning, and philosophy.
- **Creative problem-solving:**
  Outside of pure analysis, I love the hands-on process of capturing and cleaning up sound and video for live events and documentaries.

I value clear communication, respect, rigorous thinking, and work that serves a practical purpose. When I’m away from my desk, I’m usually studying mathematics or statistics, exploring the natural wonders near my city, or catching up with friends.
