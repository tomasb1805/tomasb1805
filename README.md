# Hi! I am Brian

I love unraveling complex systems and figuring out how things work, whether it’s analyzing human behavior, spotting financial patterns, or taking apart the dehumidifier in my apartment.

## How I work
- **Looking under the hood:**
  I am never satisfied with being handed a solution without first understanding how the underlying factors interact at a fundamental level, and, most importantly, why the solution works.
- **Opting for reliability over flashiness:**
  I prefer clean, reliable setups over quick fixes. From organizing my own home servers to designing how information flows through my projects, I care about structure, flow control, and efficiency.
- **Continuous learning:**
  I am always teaching myself something new. I enjoy reading academic papers in my spare time and learning about new progress in several fields: psychology, sociology, neurology, economics, applied machine learning, and philosophy.
- **Creative problem-solving:**
  Outside of pure analysis, I love the hands-on process of capturing and cleaning up sound and video for live events and documentaries.

I value clear communication, respect, rigorous thinking, and work that serves a practical purpose. When I’m away from my desk, I’m usually studying mathematics or statistics, exploring the natural wonders near my city, or catching up with friends.
